Privacy Policy

SayShadow for Mac · Version 0.1 · Last updated November 24, 2026

Short version: SayShadow does not collect any personal data. Nothing you hear, say or save leaves your Mac.

What the app processes, and where

  • Sound of the app you choose. Kept in memory for the last three minutes so you can replay a sentence. It is not stored on disk unless you save a flashcard.
  • Your voice. When you repeat a sentence, the app records it from the microphone to compare your melody with the speaker's. The recording stays in memory and is saved only as part of a flashcard you choose to keep.
  • Speech recognition. Uses Apple's on-device speech recognition on your Mac. macOS may download the speech model for a language once from Apple.
  • Flashcards. Written to an Anki deck file in a folder you choose, or sent to Anki running on the same Mac (AnkiConnect, address 127.0.0.1).
  • Settings. Window position, whether you have seen the introduction, and the chosen deck folder are stored locally on your Mac.

What the app does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs. The app does not send data to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

System Audio Recording lets the app hear the app you pick. Microphone lets it record your attempt. You can turn both off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security.

Kurzfassung auf Deutsch
